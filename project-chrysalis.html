Chrysalis

Chrysalis Music Festival is a musical festival centred around the convergence of the neo-classical and ambient genres. Centring emergence and transformation, this unique musical experience invites attendees into a dreamlike environment, where feelings of nostalgia and emotional metamorphosis, erupt into catharsis.
