King Protea

King Protea is a wine and snack bar that celebrates the rich multicultural diversity of South Africa, with an environment  that has been carefully crafted to encourage connection  and community. With flavours, spaces, and designs curated to blend the many cultures in South Africa, King Protea is a place where everyone can feel at home. Every detail, from the food to the design, reflects a mission to celebrate culture and bring people together; through good food, good wine, and meaningful conversation.
